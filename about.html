The Problem

Indian businesses are making
expensive decisions blind

Every morning, a business owner in Haridwar, Surat, or Ludhiana opens the same generic newspaper, checks the same overcrowded WhatsApp groups, and calls their supplier — hoping to get a fair price. This is how lakhs of crores in raw material purchases happen every day in India. Manually. Blindly. Without intelligence.

No personalized business news
National newspapers cover Sensex and policy. They don't tell a plastic moulder in Ahmedabad that HDPE imports from China just dropped 8%, or a textile trader in Surat that cotton futures are signaling a 3-week high. Generic news misses the signals that actually matter.

Prices for MS Sheet, copper, HDPE, diesel, and cement change daily. There is no single, reliable, free source that shows an Indian fabricator today's rate in their city — with yesterday's comparison, trend direction, and a 30-day history chart.
